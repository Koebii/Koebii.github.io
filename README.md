# Vorlesungsmitschriften

Sammlung von Vorlesungsmitschriften, die ich über die Jahre getippt habe. 

### FS 2022
- [Wahrscheinlichkeitstheorie](https://koebii.github.io//Wahrscheinlichkeitstheorie.pdf). Elementary probability theory. Sequences of independent random variables and introduction to stochastic processes (Markov chains). Notes are in English

### FS 2024
- [Stochastische Prozesse](https://koebii.github.io//Stochastische%20Prozesse.pdf). Einführung in Markov-Ketten (Deutsch).
- [Kinetic Theory](https://koebii.github.io//Kinetic%20Theory.pdf). Introduction to the analysis used to model kinetic theory. A bit of derivations from a physics perspective as well (English). *Not proofread!*

### HS 2024
- [Einführung in die Topologie](https://koebii.github.io//Topologie.pdf). Einführung in die grundlegen Begriffe der Topologie und eine Einführung in die algebraische Topologie (Deutsch).

### FS 2025
- [Elliptic PDEs: Theory and Applications](https://koebii.github.io//Elliptic%20PDEs.pdf). An advanced course in elliptic PDEs (English). *A lot of typos, be careful!*

### HS 2025
- [Stochastic Analysis](https://koebii.github.io//SA.pdf). First part of the Master's lecture in probability theory (English). *These notes are extremely bad and contain a lot of typos!*

### FS 2026
- [Selected Topics in Probability Theory](https://koebii.github.io//main.pdf). Second part of the Master's lecture in probability theory (English). *Notes are incomplete and not revised, hence contain a lot of typos as well.*
